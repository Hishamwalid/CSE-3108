Classic Margherita Pizza

A timeless Italian recipe from Naples

Prep: 20 min · Cook: 12 min · Serves: 4

About this recipe

The Classic Margherita pizza is the king of all Neapolitan pizzas. Named after Queen Margherita of Savoy in 1889, it carries the colors of the Italian flag — red tomato, white mozzarella, and green basil. It is made with a simple, slow-risen dough, San Marzano tomatoes, fresh mozzarella cheese, fragrant basil leaves, and a drizzle of extra-virgin olive oil. The combination of these few, honest ingredients creates a perfect balance of flavors that is loved all over the world.

Ingredients

500g bread flour 325ml warm water
10g salt 7g dried yeast
1 tsp sugar 200g fresh mozzarella cheese
200g tomato sauce a handful of fresh basil leaves
1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil a pinch of dried oregano

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt, sugar, and warm water together in a large bowl.
  2. Knead the dough well for 8 to 10 minutes until it is smooth and elastic.
  3. Cover the dough and let it rise for 1 hour until it has doubled in size.
  4. Preheat your oven and baking stone to 450°F or higher for at least 30 minutes.
  5. Roll or stretch the dough into a thin, flat circle about 12 inches wide.
  6. Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce over the base, leaving a small border.
  7. Add torn pieces of fresh mozzarella cheese evenly across the sauce.
  8. Sprinkle a pinch of dried oregano over the top for extra flavor.
  9. Bake in the hot oven for 7 to 10 minutes until the crust is golden and bubbly.
  10. Finish with fresh basil leaves and a drizzle of olive oil, then slice and serve.

Tips: Always use a very hot oven — the higher the heat, the crispier the crust. Do not overload the dough with toppings; a thin layer keeps the base light. Add the fresh basil after baking so the leaves stay green and fragrant. Let the pizza rest for 2 minutes before slicing so the cheese sets.
